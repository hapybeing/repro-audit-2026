## Limitations (read first)
- Small sample: n = 5 papers (batches/batch2_counts_rule4.json). These counts support no rate claims about any journal, field or repository.
- The two agents are not independent human replications. Agents A and B are separate runs with no shared context (B never saw A's logs, diffs or outputs), but they use the same underlying model family, so they can share blind spots.
- 07: the fixest version of the run of record (0.12.1) was chosen after seeing the output of current CRAN fixest 0.14.2 (CHANGELOG_METHOD.md #6b). Both runs are kept and the 0.14.2 class is reported as the alternative.
- 07: the main article is paywalled and its Table 2 was not compared. The targets come from the supplementary material (pre-run decision #4, batches/batch2_class_decisions.md), which could not be downloaded, so the supplement values rest on screenshots taken during pre-registration (hashes in batches/batch2_prereg_hashes.txt) and a browser re-reading by agent A; agent B could not reopen the supplement (papers/07_JH9DBC/B/outputs/supplement_reopen_attempt_B.md).
- 10: the article and its supplement could not be saved as files (Sage blocks scripted downloads), so no hash of either exists; the data-statement finding rests on two full readings and transcripts (papers/10_6MLKJZ/DATA_STATEMENT.md).
- 09: Study 1 (targets T5 and T6) was not checked at all. Its inputs sit behind registration at ELIPSS, and the audit does not register anywhere (PROTOCOL.md section 9).
- 06: the headline AME for Democratic white women sits near p = .05 (paper .054; reproduced AME p 0.0569, papers/06_VSV5DM/A/outputs/targets_extracted.csv), so small numerical differences can move it across a threshold.
- Robustness: pre-registered checks change the significance pattern in 06 and 09 (all three checks in each; batches/batch2_results_rule4.csv). Robustness never changes a class (PROTOCOL.md section 7).
- Absence searches can show absence only within what they searched (papers/08_SQRZR8/ABSENCE_SEARCHES.md, papers/09_4U8CTG/ABSENCE_SEARCHES.md, papers/10_6MLKJZ/ABSENCE_SEARCHES.md).
- The class reading in CHANGELOG_METHOD.md #6a (an N-only or p-only mismatch with every estimate matching is C, not D) was made after results were known; counts are shown both ways below.
- No author was contacted. A class C means "could not be fully checked from the materials the article points to". It does not mean the paper is wrong.

## Plain-language summary
Of five randomly drawn papers, one (07) reproduced exactly from the deposited materials, and only with the version of a statistics package that was current when its code was written; with today's version one sample size differs slightly. In the other four, every estimate that could be computed matched the paper, but one printed number could not be produced as pre-registered (a p-value in one paper, a printed sample size in two others), or half the paper's evidence sits behind a registration wall (one paper). No paper showed a discrepancy that changes its conclusion. In two papers the pre-registered robustness checks weaken the significance of results that sit close to the 0.05 threshold (batches/batch2_class_decisions.md). Two coding slips were verified, neither touching a headline estimate. None of this says any paper is wrong: these are statements about what the deposited materials allow an outsider to check.
