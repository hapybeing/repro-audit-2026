# Diffs, 13_DX5T9G agent A

1. `00_Run_it_all.R.diff`: `registerDoMC(cores=7)` to `registerDoMC(cores=1)`. Environment fix allowed by PREREG section 2 and required by the batch 3 brief (memory). Estimates do not depend on the worker count because `run_models` calls `set.seed(1230)` before every multinom fit and draw.

2. `03_Estimate_main_models_FALLBACK.diff` (labelled deviation, PREREG section 2 fallback). The full `00_Run it all.R` with cores=1 was OOM-killed by the kernel inside `03` (anon RSS 3.04 GB at kill; outputs/run_logs/run_full_oom_kernel.txt, exit 137). The machine had about 2 to 3 GB available, shared with other agents. The fallback copy of `03` keeps every line from the data read through `set.seed(1230)`, `multinom(...)`, `mvrnorm(n=5000, ...)` and the `store_coefficients` adply block unchanged, for all five models and both countries. It removes only the simulation of substantive effects (deposited lines 69 to 137), the treatment-moderation block that uses those simulations (141 to 162) and the CSV export of the simulations (177 to 187); `out` keeps only `Coefficients`, and the Rdata is saved as `Estimates-Main-fallback.Rdata`. No random numbers are consumed between `set.seed(1230)` and `mvrnorm`, so `Mean`, `SD`, `pvalue`, `N` are the same as the full script would give on this platform.

3. `00_fallback_runner.diff`: new runner = lines 1 to 64 of `00_Run it all.R` verbatim (with fix 1), then `01_Prepare data.R` unchanged, the fallback `03`, a copy of `Estimates-Main-fallback.Rdata` to `Estimates-Main.Rdata`, `05_Make main tables.R` unchanged, and the Table_2/Table_3 copies.
