## Audit note

The Study 1 data are available to any researcher who registers with ELIPSS. Not depositing them follows the authors' agreement with the data provider. The audit did not run the Study 1 targets only because, under a rule fixed before any run (PROTOCOL.md section 9; batches/batch2_class_decisions.md item 2), it does not register or sign in anywhere. The class follows that pre-registered rule and is unchanged: main class C, alternative class A on the openly available Study 2 targets T1 to T4. Class C here means the audit could not check Study 1 without registering; it does not mean any material is missing from the authors' side or that the paper is wrong.
