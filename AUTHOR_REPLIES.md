# Author replies and addenda

How replies from authors are handled after the 12 October 2026 notification (outreach_log.md). This file sets out process only. It does not change any result, class, count, hash or pre-registered target.

## Publishing replies
- Nothing from an author's correspondence is published without the authors' agreement.
- Where authors ask for their reply to be added, it goes in full in `papers/<id>/AUTHOR_RESPONSE.md`, with contact details removed, and the paper's line in FINAL_REPORT.md points to it.
- Where a summary is used instead, the authors see the exact wording before it is posted, and can ask for it to be verbatim, summarised or left out.
- Any audit note placed under a reply states facts from the paper's folder only.

## Dates
- 26 October 2026 ends the window before any paper-level discussion on social media. It is not a deadline for corrections or for additional materials, which are welcome at any time.

## Addenda when authors supply materials
- If authors deposit or share materials that were missing, the audit reruns that paper's pre-registered targets (`papers/<id>/targets.json`, values and rounding unchanged) under the same PROTOCOL.md procedure, with environment-only changes recorded as diffs.
- The rerun is published as a dated addendum in `papers/<id>/ADDENDUM_<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`, with its outputs written by script, and the paper's line in FINAL_REPORT.md shows the addendum class next to the original class.
- The original class and the headline counts stay as pre-registered and archived (v1.0, Zenodo concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23293097). Addenda are listed below and noted beside the counts, never folded into them.
- Materials in a public deposit are preferred, since others can then reuse them; files shared directly are also accepted and are described, not republished, unless the authors agree.
